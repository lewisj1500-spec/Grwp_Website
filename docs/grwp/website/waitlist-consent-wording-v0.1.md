# Grwp waitlist — consent wording

Version 0.1, prepared 20 October 2026; live date recorded when Lewis approves the deploy.

This is the exact wording a visitor sees when they join the waitlist. It appears
in two places on grwp.app: the hero form and the closing form. Both post to the
same Formspree endpoint.

## Field

Placeholder: `you@email.com`

## Button

`Join the waitlist`

## Directly under the form

`One email when we launch. Unsubscribe anytime. Privacy`

"Privacy" is a link to `/website-privacy`.

## After a successful sign-up

`You're on the list. We'll email you once, when Grwp launches. Changed your mind? Email privacy@grwp.app and we'll remove you.`
