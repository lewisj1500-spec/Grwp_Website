Waitlist

If you join, we keep your email address, the time you signed up and the form wording you saw, so that we can send you one email when Grwp launches. We delete it 30 days after launch, or sooner if you email privacy@grwp.app or unsubscribe. Legal reason: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Visitor logs

Our web host, Vercel, keeps technical logs of visits, which can include your IP address, for up to an hour, to keep the site secure and working. Legal reason: legitimate interests.

Who holds it

Vercel, our web host; Cloudflare, which delivers and protects the site and so sees your IP address when you visit; and Formspree, which runs our waitlist form. All three are based in the US. Vercel and Cloudflare are certified under the UK–US data bridge, and Formspree uses standard contractual clauses. We use no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and we never sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email privacy@grwp.app. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
